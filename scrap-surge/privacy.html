Scrap Surge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Scrap Surge ("the game") is made by FlouLox Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. If you have a question or a request, email flouloxstudios@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (scrap, gems, modules, paint jobs, missions, settings and so on) is saved on your device only. We do not receive it. Uninstalling the game, or using Settings → Reset progress, deletes it. Reminders are planned on your device too, and you can turn them off in Settings.

What we collect

InformationWhy
Your call sign (the name you choose), a random pilot ID created by the game, your scores (furthest sector, debris salvaged, best combo, best Endless run and your score in this week's contest) and your ship's hull tier and paint jobTo show the online leaderboards and pilot cards. The pilot ID is random: it is not linked to your name, email, phone number or any account.
Technical request data (such as your IP address) when the game talks to our leaderboard serverNeeded to deliver the leaderboard and to protect the server from abuse. We do not store it with your scores or your pilot ID. Our hosting provider may keep short-lived connection logs.

We do not ask for your real name, email address, contacts, location or photos, and we do not sell personal information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded video ads (you choose to watch one in return for an in-game reward). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent message; you can change your choice at any time with Settings → Ad privacy choices in the game.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases (gems, packs, paint jobs and the season pass) are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. We receive only a confirmation of what was bought (a purchase token and product ID) so the game can deliver it.

Who we share information with

  • Other players see your call sign, scores, hull and paint job on the leaderboards.
  • People you choose: a friend challenge code contains your call sign and Endless score, and an invite mentions your sector. These only go where you send them.
  • Google (AdMob) for ads, as described above.
  • Google Play for purchases.
  • Our hosting provider, which runs the leaderboard server for us.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboards run. Weekly boards are deleted automatically after eight weeks. You can ask us to delete your leaderboard entry at any time (see below).

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR), you can ask to access, correct or delete your information, object to its use, or withdraw consent. To delete your leaderboard entry, email us your call sign and your pilot ID, which is shown at the bottom of the game's Settings. The game posts your scores again as you keep playing, so reset your progress or uninstall the game first. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on: providing the service you asked for (the leaderboards and purchases), our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure, and your consent (personalised ads).

Children

Scrap Surge is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age for consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version; important changes will be announced in the game.
