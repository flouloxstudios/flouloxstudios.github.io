The core experience

Salvage, upgrade, explore, conquer.

A portrait space-salvage game for phones. Tap debris for scrap while your drones fire on their own, grow your ship and squadron, and push through sector after sector until its guardian shows itself.

Inside the game

Every sector has a story.

Real moments and real art from the current build of Scrap Surge.

01 / Guardians

The sector fights back.

Fill a sector's salvage quota and the alarms start. Its guardian arrives with a name, a pattern of its own and 45 seconds on the clock.

  • Interrupt its lasers before they land.
  • Clear the minions and break its shields.
  • Hit the core and warp further out.

Seven hulls · 43 paint jobs · up to eight drones

02 / Progression

Build your operation.

Shards level up your four modules, and your ship grows through seven hulls as they do. Stripes, camouflage, chrome and animated plasma make it yours.

Start with one drone. Collect parts from derelicts and guardians, and the next one assembles itself, up to a squadron of eight.

What's new

Every world plays different now.

The November 2026 update, in short.

  • World rulesIce shells on Glacier Tomb, sandstorms over the Dune Sea, a beating heart in Obsidian Heart, turrets on Machine Prime: each world changes how the debris behaves, with its own music and weather.
  • PerksEvery fifth module level you pick one of two perks, from chain-cutting crits to drones that hunt gold. Your ship, your build.
  • Better guardian fightsA few quick taps on the core now stop a guardian's laser. Deep and elite guardians come armoured, regenerating, swift or splitting, and they shatter when they fall.
  • Daily runAn endless run with a new twist every day, the same for everyone. Your first run each day pays gems.
  • Tips when you need themA shorter first flight, and the game now pauses to point things out the first time you meet them: the red canister, the SURGE dial, a guardian's laser.
  • Move to a new phoneSettings makes a transfer code for your progress. Enter it on your new phone and carry on where you left off.
  • Ads you chooseAds are optional rewarded videos. You decide when to watch one, and you get something for it.
  • No paid chanceNothing sold for money or gems is left to chance. Scan tickets are only earned by playing.
  • No account neededProgress stays on your device. The leaderboard needs only a call sign, a random pilot ID and your scores.
