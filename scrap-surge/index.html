Overview

About Scrap Surge

Space is full of junk, and junk is full of scrap. Salvage it, collect the shards that level up your four modules, and watch your ship grow through five hulls.

Every sector has a world, a story and a guardian. Fill the quota, beat the guardian against the clock, and warp further out along a route that never ends.

  • Salvage & progress

    Break debris for scrap and shards, and upgrade your ship and a squadron of up to eight drones.

  • 17 worlds, then further

    Each world has its own contact, story and objective before the guardian shows itself.

  • Sector guardians

    Interrupt lasers, clear minions, break shields and hit the core within 45 seconds.

  • Always something on

    A daily trial, weekly events, a season every six weeks and Endless runs to challenge friends.

Features

More than a tap

Sector guardians

Fill the quota and the alarms start. Interrupt its lasers, clear its minions, break its shields and hit the core before time runs out.

Five hulls, 43 paint jobs

Your ship grows as your modules level up. Stripes, camouflage, chrome and animated plasma make it yours.

A squadron of eight

Start with one drone. Collect parts from derelicts and guardians, and the next one assembles itself.

Salvage scans

Lock on, draw a capsule in, and see what it's carrying. Every tenth scan is at least Epic.

Always something on

A daily trial, weekly events, a new season every six weeks, and Endless runs you can challenge friends to beat.

The route

17 worlds, then further

Each world has its own contact, story and guardian. After Machine Prime, the route just keeps going.

  1. 01EarthEarth launch control marks the first salvage corridor.
  2. 02Scrap BeltThe first scraps drift beyond Earth's launch lane.
  3. 03Copper MoonRefinery moon salvage coordinates found.
  4. 04Verdant WreckOld wrecks bloom with rich salvage.
  5. 05Ion ReefBlue storms charge every loose plate.
  6. 06Aurora ForgeThe forge glow hints someone came before.
  7. 07Signal NexusThe pulsing signal was a beacon.
  8. 08Glacier TombA starliner sleeps under the ice, and the ice is cracking.
  9. 09Dune SeaAncient ships lie half-buried in an ocean of sand.
  10. 10Coral AbyssLiving reefs glow over sunken wrecks.
  11. 11Obsidian HeartBlack glass and rivers of magma. The planet's heart is beating.
  12. 12Amber MaelstromOne storm bigger than worlds, and a single platform riding it.
  13. 13Crystal BloomCrystals the size of towers, humming in harmony.
  14. 14Sulfur WastesVolcanoes, refineries, and a smell you'll never forget.
  15. 15Twilight DivideHalf fire, half ice. Life clings to the line between.
  16. 16Shattered MoonA broken moon, held together by scaffolds and stubbornness.
  17. 17Machine PrimeA whole planet built as a machine. It has been waiting for you.
