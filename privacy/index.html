This website

This website has no accounts, cookies, analytics or ads, and its fonts are served from this site rather than a third party. The demo on the Scrap Surge page runs in your browser, and nothing you do in it is saved or sent to us.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the site and keep it secure, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The domain is managed by Cloudflare, which answers DNS lookups for it and may forward email sent to it, as described in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

The contact form on the home page doesn't send anything itself: it opens your own email app with your message filled in, and nothing is sent until you press send there. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.
