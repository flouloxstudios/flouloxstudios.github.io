About us

Built to play fair

FlouLox Studios is an independent studio making games for your phone. We build the kind of games we want to play ourselves: instant to start, with enough depth to keep you coming back. Free-to-play doesn't have to feel like a trap; here's how that works in Scrap Surge.

  • Ads you choose

    Ads are optional rewarded videos. You decide when to watch one, and you get something for it.

  • No paid chance

    Nothing sold for money or gems is left to chance. Scan tickets are only earned by playing, and offers show exactly what's inside.

  • No account needed

    Your progress stays on your device. The leaderboard needs only a call sign you pick, a random pilot ID and your scores.
